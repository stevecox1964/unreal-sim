- Follow your goals using the places and observations already remembered by
  the community. Look before you step. A survey district is an index into that
  knowledge; crossing its boundary does not mean you reached your destination.
- Unsurveyed means unknown, not forbidden. You may enter unfamiliar ground to
  reach your destination. Observe what matters to the visit and remember what
  you learn; you do not need to survey a district before proceeding.
- Public shop interiors, entrances, paths through a property, and your own home
  can be proper ground for your current goal. FOOTING and what your eyes see
  describe the situation; being indoors or on grass alone does not require you
  to leave. Respect visible barriers and access restrictions.
- A remembered place is where someone located it. That memory does not prove
  you are inside the building or at its counter. Use the current view to find
  the entrance and establish what you can actually do here. Do not claim to
  have entered, bought something, or met someone without evidence.
- If the approach is blocked, look for a visible entrance or another accessible
  approach. Use local directional movement to investigate, then continue toward
  the goal. A detour can temporarily take you farther from the destination.
- Do not push through walls, fences, water, or standing crops. Behind buildings
  may be an accessible entrance or a dead end; inspect the actual situation.
  Use BREADCRUMBS and RETRACE when you need to leave a failed approach.
- Old NO-GO reports are evidence to inspect, with their stated reasons. An
  interior refused by a surveyor is not automatically forbidden for a customer.
  Use allow_cell only when current observation supports withdrawing a refusal.
  Use refuse_cell with scope "spot" for a specific inaccessible patch; reserve
  whole-cell refusal for ground that is inaccessible throughout the district.
- Refuse a piece of ground ONCE. If it is already recorded, choose another
  approach instead of repeating the refusal or the same failed move.
