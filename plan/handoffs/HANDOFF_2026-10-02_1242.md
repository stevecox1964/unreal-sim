## Decisions locked in this session

- Tile = 3 m (`world_grid.TILE_CM`), indexed from the logical grid origin. 10×10 tiles per grid cell.
- A place = a set of tiles keyed by the normalized name, shared by all owners. This unions the
  duplicate Don's Donuts rows. The old `owned_place_cells` / `place_cells` rows are not migrated.
- Lazy seed: the first time a place resolves, seed tiles cover the old box (3×3).
- Growth is a body fact. A tile touching the bound place (schedule block place, else travel
  destination) is added as `stood`. Nothing is added while stuck. Cap: 100 tiles per place.
- Arrival = standing on a tile of the place. At-place roaming may reach one tile past the edge, so
  the place can grow inward.
- `speak_to` / `inspect_object` farther than 3.5 m: walk to the 3 m standoff point, park the
  action, and deliver it from a bridge-only pulse after the body faces the target. It gives up after
  6 ticks. Targets with no position pass through.
- The listener is not turned. It turns when it answers. Hearing stays 12 m.
